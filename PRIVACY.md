## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information directly from you:

**Account information.**
- Email address (required for sign-in and password reset)
- Display name (shown alongside your reviews unless you mark them anonymous)
- Password, stored as a one-way salted hash (we cannot read your password)

**Membership context.**
- The invite code you used to register (tracks who your inviter was, used only
  for admin-side auditing)
- Your home metro (set by your invite code; determines which contractors you
  can see)
- Whether you have administrative privileges (set manually by an
  administrator)

**Content you create.**
- Contractor listings you submit (business name, description, trade
  categories, phone number, email, website, zip code, service area, self-
  reported attestations such as licensed/insured/business type)
- Reviews you write (text, recommend/do-not-recommend flag, optional star
  rating, optional cost range, optional job description, optional anonymous
  flag)
- Reports/flags you submit on contractors or reviews (the reason text and
  target id)

**Device information.**
- An Expo Push token tied to your device, stored so we can send push
  notifications if you grant permission (e.g. "Your contractor submission was
  approved")
- Standard request metadata logged by our infrastructure provider (IP
  address, user agent, request timestamps) for security and abuse prevention

We do NOT collect:
- Your location via GPS or any location services
- Photos, camera content, microphone audio, contacts, or calendar data
- Biometric data
- Information from third-party social networks
- Data via advertising identifiers or third-party trackers (we do not run
  advertisements)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

- To authenticate you and maintain your session
- To gate access to the directory to verified, invited members only
- To attribute reviews and contractor submissions to you (or display them as
  anonymous if you so choose)
- To show you contractors whose service area covers your home metro
- To send push notifications you have opted into, such as when a contractor
  you submitted is approved by an administrator
- To enable administrators to moderate content, verify license claims, and
  resolve reports
- To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and misuse of the service
- To comply with applicable law

We do not sell your personal information, share it with advertisers, or use
it to build advertising profiles.

## 4. Information About Third Parties (Contractors)

The app records information about contractors (e.g. business name, contact
methods, license status, work quality reviews). This information is provided
by members about businesses they have personally interacted with. We do not
verify the accuracy of submitted information except where we mark a license
as "Verified by admin," in which case an administrator has cross-checked the
license against a public registry.

If you are a contractor and believe information about your business posted in
the app is inaccurate or defamatory, contact [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS] and we will
review and respond within a reasonable time.

## 5. Who We Share Your Information With

- **Law enforcement or government agencies**, only when required by valid

We do NOT share your information with advertisers, data brokers, analytics
companies, or marketing platforms.

## 7. Data Retention

- Account information: retained as long as your account is active. Deleted on
  request, subject to limited retention for legal compliance and abuse
  prevention.
- Reviews and contractor submissions: retained indefinitely as part of the
  community record, even after your account is deleted. Reviewer
  identification is anonymized on account deletion.
- Push tokens: retained while your account is active and the token is valid.
- Server access logs: retained for up to 90 days for security purposes.

## 8. Security

We protect your data via:

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS for all client-server communication)
- Encryption at rest (provided by our database host)
- Row-level access policies that prevent users from reading data belonging to
  other members
- Password hashing using industry-standard algorithms
- Limited administrative access on a need-to-know basis

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your
information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.
